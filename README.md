## Project Overview

This project presents a structured **Technology Risk & GRC assessment** of the **Tata Electronics Cybersecurity Incident (2026)** using publicly available information.

Rather than simply summarizing the incident, this case study demonstrates how a **Technology Risk Consultant** would assess a real-world cybersecurity event through a governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) lens.

The assessment follows a consulting-style methodology inspired by industry practices and aligns the findings with:

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (Annex A)
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0

# Methodology

The assessment follows a structured 12-step consulting methodology.

1. Company Background
2. Incident Overview
3. Timeline Analysis
4. Data Exposure Assessment
5. Business Impact Assessment
6. Company Response Assessment
7. Risk Register Development
8. Root Cause Analysis
9. Control Gap Assessment
10. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Mapping
11. NIST CSF 2.0 Mapping
12. Executive Recommendations & Risk Treatment Plan

# Key Findings

### Business Strengths

- Effective Incident Response
- Business Continuity Maintained
- No Reported Operational Downtime

### Potential Risk Areas

- Information Classification
- Data Protection
- Continuous Monitoring
- Detection Capability
- Privacy Protection
- Third-Party Risk Governance

### Controls That Could Not Be Confirmed

- Access Control
- MFA
- Vulnerability Management
- Logging Effectiveness
- Encryption at Rest
- Supplier Security Controls
